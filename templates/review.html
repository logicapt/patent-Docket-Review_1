Paste page content — no docket file needed
  1. Open the actual case in your normal browser and sign in there if required.
  2. Once the docket is visible, click its page, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C. Include the case caption, number, court, column headings and docket rows.
  3. Paste below with Ctrl+V, supply the case URL, then analyze. Your browser can include the copied table formatting automatically.

Only the content you paste is read. No clipboard permission, file upload, website password or cookie is needed.

This checks the identity inside your copy; it does not independently verify the website, authenticity or freshness. Content is processed in memory and cleared from this input when submitted. Your operating system may retain clipboard history. If the docket is not visible in your normal browser, this option cannot retrieve it.
